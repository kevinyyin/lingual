# Which signs the camera reads (translator, 2026-10-24)

How this was measured: every WLASL dictionary video of the 160 hand-checked signs that starts and ends with the hands
at rest (596 attempts, 2 to 7 per sign) was replayed through the app as it runs now (the camera chooses among the 160
signs, drops the hands going back down, and opens the picker when a sign is read less than 95% surely) at 15
frames/s, one sign per turn. A scripted signer picks the right sign when the picker offers it and otherwise presses
"None of these". **Right** = the turn says exactly that sign, straight away or after picking.

Overall: right 64% (43% straight away, 21% via the picker), a wrong word spoken 15%, lost at "None of these" 21%.

Read it with care: with 2 to 7 attempts per sign, one miss moves a sign by 15 to 50 points. These are fluent dictionary
signers, each signing one variant (the avatar's clips come from these same videos), not a learner on a webcam.

## Lean on these: right in at least 3 of 4 attempts (60 signs)

- **social:** MEET, NAME, NO, PLEASE, THANK-YOU
- **people:** HEARING, FAMILY, FATHER, DEAF, FRIEND
- **question:** HOW, WHEN, WHICH, WHY, WHO
- **action:** READ, OPEN, REMEMBER, SLEEP, KNOW, WANT, THINK, BUY, GO, LEARN, AGAIN, MORE, WRITE, FEEL
- **negation:** NEVER
- **time:** TIME, MORNING, HOUR, NIGHT, WEEK, TODAY, YESTERDAY
- **place:** HOSPITAL, HERE, BUS, RESTAURANT, BATHROOM, HOME
- **food:** BREAD, THIRSTY, EAT, APPLE, EXPENSIVE, SANDWICH, HUNGRY, SUGAR
- **feeling:** SICK, SAD, SCARED
- **describe:** OLD, WRONG, SAME, ALL
- **number:** THREE, EIGHT

Also right every time, but only 1 or 2 attempts: DRINK, FAST, MILK, TRY, WELCOME, WE, HURT, TWO, FAVORITE.

## Usable: right in half to three quarters (45 signs)

- **social:** SORRY, OK, NICE
- **people:** BABY, PERSON, DOCTOR, YOUR, MY
- **action:** WAIT, CLOSE, HAVE, LOVE, SIGN, CHANGE, NEED, WORK, CAN, FINISH, UNDERSTAND, LIKE, SIT, PAY, SEE
- **time:** DAY, TOMORROW, LATER, NOW
- **place:** STORE, CAR
- **food:** COFFEE, HOT, COOKIE, COLD, PIZZA, BIG, CHEESE
- **feeling:** EXCITED, ANGRY, TIRED, HAPPY
- **describe:** EASY, DIFFERENT, RIGHT
- **number:** SEVEN, ONE

## Avoid on the signing side: right in under half (37 signs)

- **social:** YES, GOODBYE
- **people:** THEY, YOU, HE-SHE, MOTHER, I
- **question:** WHAT, WHERE
- **action:** MAKE, STOP, HELP, GIVE, COME, TALK
- **negation:** NOT, NONE
- **time:** SOON, MINUTE
- **place:** SCHOOL
- **food:** CUP, MONEY, WATER, TEA, SMALL, CAKE
- **feeling:** BAD, FINE, GOOD
- **describe:** READY, HARD, MANY, SLOW, NEW
- **number:** FOUR, SIX, NINE

Can never be read (ASL Citizen has no such sign): FIVE, FOOD, FORGET, HOW-MANY, TEN.

Not tested: EXCUSE-ME, HELLO, JUICE, LOOK. HELLO's one video does not start at rest, so it could not be replayed as
a turn; read as a whole clip, the camera gets it right. The others have no video on this machine.
